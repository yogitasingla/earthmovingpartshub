1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Earthmoving Parts Hub ("we", "us", or "our") manages information in connection with your use of our website located at earthmovingpartshub.com (the "Website").

This policy is prepared in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and with awareness of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) of India.

By accessing or using our Website, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please refrain from using this Website.

3. What Our Website Does

Our website is a static, informational product showcase. Its primary functions are:

  • Displaying product image galleries categorised as OEM, Aftermarket, and Genuine JCB Spare Parts
  • Providing a downloadable PDF product catalogue — freely accessible to all visitors; no registration is required
  • Sharing our business contact information (address, phone, email, working hours)
  • Offering a contact/inquiry form for trade enquiries from prospective buyers or business partners
Important: Our website is a product catalogue display platform only. There is no online store, no shopping cart, no checkout, and no payment processing on this website. All commercial transactions, if any, are conducted offline through direct communication.

4. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to respond to your business enquiry. We collect personal information in the following situations:

4.1 Contact / Inquiry Form

When you voluntarily submit an inquiry using our contact form, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if provided)
  • Your message or enquiry content

This information is submitted voluntarily by you and is used solely to respond to your business enquiry. Submission of the contact form constitutes your consent to us processing this information for the stated purpose.

4.2 Automatically Collected Technical Data

Like all websites, our web server may automatically record basic technical data such as:

  • Your browser type and version
  • General geographic region (country/city level) via IP address
  • Pages visited and time spent on the Website
  • Referring URL (the page you came from)

This data is collected in standard server logs for security and diagnostic purposes only. It is not linked to your personal identity and is not used for marketing.

5. Information We Do NOT Collect

We are committed to data minimalism. We explicitly do not collect:

We do NOT collect the following:
  • Customer account credentials or login information — there is no user registration or login system on this website
  • Payment details, credit/debit card numbers, or banking information — no financial transactions occur on this website
  • Purchase history or order records — no e-commerce functionality exists
  • Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI) as defined under Indian IT Rules 2011, including passwords, financial information, health data, biometric data, or sexual orientation
  • Government identification numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, etc.)
  • Location data beyond general region (no GPS or precise location tracking)
  • Social media credentials or third-party account information

6. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through the contact form is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your trade inquiry or request for quotation
  • To communicate product availability, pricing, or specifications as requested
  • To follow up on your business enquiry if you have requested a callback or further details

We do not use your information for automated profiling, targeted advertising, or any purpose beyond directly responding to your enquiry. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party libraries and services. These are loaded from external servers and their respective privacy policies apply:

  • Google Fonts — When your browser loads our website, it may make a request to Google's servers to retrieve font files (Open Sans, Poppins, Raleway). Google may collect basic technical information (IP address) from this request. Refer to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Bootstrap (via local files) — Loaded locally from our server. No data is sent to external parties.
  • AOS (Animate On Scroll) — Loaded locally. No data is transmitted externally.
  • GLightbox — Used to display product images in a lightbox viewer. Loaded locally. No external data sharing.
  • Swiper.js — Used for image carousels. Loaded locally. No external data sharing.
  • GitHub Pages — Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server access logs as part of their infrastructure. Refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

We do not integrate any social media tracking pixels, Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising networks on this website.

8. Cookies & Browser Storage

Our website uses minimal, functional cookies only. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytical cookies that profile your behaviour.

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser, used to maintain basic page functionality (e.g., Bootstrap tab state).
  • No Persistent Tracking Cookies: We do not set long-lived tracking cookies on your device.
  • No Third-Party Ad Cookies: We do not use any advertising or remarketing cookie systems.

You may control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to browse our product catalogue or download the PDF catalogue.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you submit through our contact form from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Hosting on a secure platform (GitHub Pages)
  • Transmitting contact form data over secure channels
  • Limiting access to received enquiry data to authorised personnel only

However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. By submitting an inquiry form, you acknowledge these inherent risks.

10. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only for as long as necessary to address your enquiry or as required for legitimate business record-keeping purposes. Once an enquiry is resolved and no ongoing business relationship exists, personal data associated with that enquiry will be deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period.

We do not retain personal data for longer than is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

11. Your Rights Under Indian Law

Under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the emerging Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), you have certain rights regarding your personal data. These include:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation of whether we hold any personal data about you and receive a copy of such data.
  • Right to Correction: You may request that inaccurate or incomplete personal data be corrected or updated.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain certain records.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on your consent (e.g., contact form submission), you may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to lodge a grievance with us regarding the processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the details provided in Section 14 below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.
